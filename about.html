The House of NOIR

Born
for Presence.

NOIR FRAGRANCE was created around a simple belief: a fragrance should become more than a scent. It should become part of your identity — quietly powerful, unmistakably personal, and impossible to forget.

01

The Beginning

NOIR began with an ambition to create fragrances that feel timeless rather than temporary.

In a world where trends disappear quickly, we believe true luxury is different. It is found in craftsmanship, attention to detail and the confidence to create something that does not need to follow everyone else.

Every NOIR fragrance is imagined as a signature — something that accompanies you through important moments and quietly becomes part of your story.

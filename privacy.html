Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Our Commitment

At NOIR FRAGRANCE, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you provide when using our website, purchasing our products or communicating with our team.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used and the steps we take to protect it.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our website, we may collect information such as:

  • Name and contact information.
  • Email address and telephone number.
  • Shipping and delivery information.
  • Order and purchase information.
  • Messages or enquiries submitted through our website.
  • Basic technical information required to operate and improve our website.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Process and fulfil orders.
  • Arrange delivery of purchased products.
  • Respond to customer questions and enquiries.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Improve our products, services and website.
  • Help maintain website security.

Payments

When online payment services are introduced, payments may be processed through third-party payment providers.

NOIR FRAGRANCE does not intend to store complete payment-card details on its own website. Payment information may instead be handled directly by the relevant payment service provider.

Cookies & Technology

Our website may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies to provide functionality such as shopping carts, preferences, security and website analytics.

Information Protection

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-Party Services

Our website may eventually use trusted third-party services for functions such as payment processing, delivery, analytics, hosting, communications or customer support.

Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

Your Choices

You may contact NOIR FRAGRANCE if you have questions regarding the information we hold about you or if you wish to request appropriate access, correction or deletion of your information, subject to applicable law and legitimate business requirements.

Children

Our website is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children.

Policy Updates

NOIR FRAGRANCE may update this Privacy Policy as our website, services and business develop. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

Contact Us

If you have any privacy-related questions, please contact NOIR FRAGRANCE through our official contact page.
